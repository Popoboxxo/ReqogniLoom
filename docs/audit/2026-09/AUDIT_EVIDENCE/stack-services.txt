`sha256:f45053df0...` (built locally), while `llm-preflight` still runs the
PULLED `ghcr.io/...:1.8.0-beta.16` — i.e. the live stack is a MIX of pulled
release images and locally built dev images. Audit agents must not assume a
single image lineage.
